# Community Guidelines

Discuss papers and ideas in good faith. Do not post harassment, threats,
hateful abuse, personal information, spam, impersonation, illegal material,
or attempts to evade moderation.

You can report a comment and block its author from the comment menu. Blocking
hides that author's discussion content for your account. Pakperk may hold new
comments for review, hide content, or suspend accounts to keep discussions
safe.

Publication version: 2026-07-31. Open the published page for the complete,
current guidelines and appeal/support path.
