# Terms

Pakperk is an early paper-reading product. Paper metadata and links may come
from third parties. Do not rely on Pakperk as the sole source for academic,
medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical decisions.

If you participate in public comments, you must follow the current Community
Guidelines and may not post illegal content, harassment, threats, doxxing,
spam, impersonation, or content that violates another person's rights.
**Report comment**, **Report user**, and **Block user** are separate controls;
reporting does not itself hide content or create a block.
