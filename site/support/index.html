Monitored contact

Support and safety

Use the narrowest route for your request and share an operation ID—not a credential or a copy of harmful content.

Inside the app

  • Use Report comment for a problem with one comment, or Report user for an account-level pattern. A report is private and does not hide content or create a block.
  • Use the separate Block user action when you no longer want to see that person’s comments.
  • Use the in-app deletion action or the web deletion route to remove your account.

Contact an operator

For account access, privacy rights, copyright or legal notices, accessibility, or urgent safety issues, use the monitored address below.

Open support email

Include a request, comment, report, or account-operation ID if available. Never send an access token, refresh token, password, private key, full harmful comment, or private paper text.

Response targets

Credible threats, child-safety material, imminent self-harm, exposed credentials, and private locations are treated as critical. Doxxing, targeted harassment, hate, non-consensual intimate material, and malicious links receive high-priority review. Ordinary spam, impersonation, copyright, and abuse enter the standard queue.
