Account lifecycle

Delete your account

This route uses a fresh sign-in to request removal. It does not ask for or retain your password, refresh token, or access token.

What deletion does

  • disables Pakperk account access immediately;
  • revokes identity-provider sessions and deletes the provider identity;
  • removes your profile, Library/To Read and discovery data, private annotations/conflicts/re-anchor history, evidence cards, checkpoints, memory, owner-bound assistant history/provenance, blocks, comments, reports, and pending account-owned operations;
  • clears account-owned data from the app after it receives acceptance, including ordinary-SQLite private research caches and pending research work; and
  • retains anonymized security records for 90 days and signed deletion authority, including encrypted provider recovery coordinates, for at least 400 days and until no recoverable backup can recreate the account.

After both the 400-day minimum has elapsed and no recoverable backup can recreate the account, an operator-controlled final purge removes the deletion authority and encrypted coordinates. Account deletion cannot be undone. Shared paper metadata, normalized documents, and shared derived artifacts that are not account-owned may remain.

Having trouble?

Contact support from the address associated with your identity provider. Never send a password or token. Deletion remains available when comments or other account features are disabled.
