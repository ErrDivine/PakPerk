Be specific, civil, and useful.

Disagreement and criticism are welcome. Abuse is not. Pakperk comments are public observations about papers, not private notes.

Do not post or facilitate

  • harassment, bullying, targeted abuse, dogpiling, hate, or dehumanizing content;
  • threats, praise or instructions for violence, or encouragement of self-harm;
  • sexual exploitation, sexual content involving minors, or non-consensual intimate material;
  • private identifying information, doxxing, credential theft, or stalking;
  • illegal goods, malicious code, fraud, or instructions intended to cause real-world harm;
  • spam, repetitive promotion, deceptive links, manipulation, impersonation, or false affiliation;
  • copyright abuse or other rights violations; or attempts to evade moderation or recreate suspended accounts.

Do not paste private peer-review material, unpublished confidential work, or large copyrighted passages. Link to a source and add your own observation.

Safety controls

Use Report on the relevant comment for a rules problem. Use Block author to remove that person’s comments from your view. For urgent safety, privacy, copyright, or legal issues, contact support and provide the comment ID rather than copying harmful content.

Enforcement

Moderators may reject, hold, hide, restore, or delete comments and may suspend or reinstate accounts. Actions are audited. Repeated or severe violations can lead to suspension.
