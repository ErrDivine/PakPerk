# Pakperk Support and Safety

**Stable release URL:** `https://pakperk.app/support`

The checked repository does not embed an email address, secret, or third-party
ticket credential. A production deployment must make the stable support URL a
working HTTPS form or contact page, publish its monitored operator details, and
route submissions according to the
[moderation runbook](../runbooks/moderation.md).

In the app:

- use **Report** on a comment for spam, harassment, hate, threats, sexual
  content, privacy, impersonation, copyright, or another rules violation;
- use **Block author** when you no longer want to see that person's comments;
  and
- use the support page for account access, privacy/deletion, copyright/legal,
  accessibility, or urgent safety issues.

Support submissions should include a request, comment, report, or account
operation ID when available. Do not copy access tokens, passwords, private
keys, or an entire harmful comment into a ticket.
